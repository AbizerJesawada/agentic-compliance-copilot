Vendor Security and Compliance Requirements

All vendors must report data breaches within 72 hours.

Vendor contracts must include confidentiality clauses.

Vendors must encrypt sensitive data before storing or transmitting it.

Vendors must complete an annual independent security audit and provide the audit report to the company.

Vendors must delete customer data within 30 days after contract termination.

Vendors must maintain and test a disaster recovery plan at least once every year.

The company may terminate vendor contracts if compliance requirements are not met.
